## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** A routine delivery confirmation requires tapping through three screens, while unreliable route persistence, stale optimization, and offline failures force the driver to repeat actions or improvise instead of completing the job.
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** The CSAT of drivers with reporting tools shows that they have difficulty updating dispatchers on their deliveries through RouteLogic's current tooling.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Frontline Delivery Drivers
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** The experience of reporting delivery stops is hindered by complex, multi-step inputs and drivers report great dissatisfaction in this area.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** Remove complexity from frontline delivery driver's job reporting such that they use the tool to report deliveries as opposed to external chats.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Time taken logging compliance checks. If reporting is streamlined and kept in the tool, coordinators should be able to close the gap between the time required to log checks in our tool vs. leading competitors.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Usage of the live dispatch tool.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** The decision window would be over two months of deliveries to account for any weekly or seasonal variance. We'd  like to see reduction in compliance check time by at least 25% to proceed with iteration.
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Based on drivers' reporting of complex tooling around reporting, compliance logging lead times, and satisfaction with reporting tools, I believe that streamlining job reporting for frontline drivers will result in more adoption of reporting through RouteLogic as measured by a reduction in logging of compliance checks. I will protect the usage of the dispatch tool as a guardrail and make a go/no-go decision within 2 months.
