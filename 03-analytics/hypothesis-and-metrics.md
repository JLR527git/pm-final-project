## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** A dispatcher reassigns a route, but the driver continues following outdated instructions for 8–15 minutes. Meanwhile, delayed status updates make completed deliveries appear active on the dashboard, forcing dispatchers to rely on WhatsApp and manual communication rather than trusting RouteLogic as their operational source of truth.
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** The CSAT of dispatchers with reporting tools shows that they have difficulty reporting and that is confirmed by the length of compliance checks compared to the baseline.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Dispatch coordinators
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** There is much time spent in logging compliance checks; likely because of the use of external tools and the complexity of RouteLogic's tooling.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** Remove complexity from reporting such that they use the tool to report deliveries as opposed to using external chats.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Time taken logging compliance checks. If reporting is streamlined and kept in the tool, coordinators should be able to close the gap between the time required to log checks in our tool vs. leading competitors.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Usage of the live dispatch board. Coordinators should continue to use the board since that is the part of the product that is most used. Any change should not negatively affect that usage.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** The decision window would be over two months of deliveries to account for any weekly or seasonal variance. We'd  like to see reduction in compliance check time by at least 25% to proceed with iteration.
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Based on coordinators' reporting of complex tooling around reporting, compliance logging lead times, and satisfaction with reporting tools, I believe that streamlining job reporting for dispatchers will result in more adoption of reporting through RouteLogic as measured by a reduction in logging of compliance checks. I will protect the usage of the dispatch tool as a guardrail and make a go/no-go decision within 2 months.
