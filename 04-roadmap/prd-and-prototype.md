# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · RouteLogic Velocity

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer + 1 CS lead

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** Dispatch coordinator
- **Primary metric:** Time taken logging compliance checks. If reporting is streamlined and kept in the tool, coordinators should be able to close the gap between the time required to log checks in our tool vs. leading competitors.
- **Moment of misery:** A dispatcher reassigns a route, but the driver continues following outdated instructions for 8–15 minutes. Meanwhile, delayed status updates make completed deliveries appear active on the dashboard, forcing dispatchers to rely on WhatsApp and manual communication rather than trusting RouteLogic as their operational source of truth.
- **Guardrail:** Usage of the live dispatch tool

## Scoring

| B1 One-Click Compliance Checklist | 5 | 2 | Quick Win | Now | The largest predicted benefit to compliance and reportng time |

| B5 Step Progress Indicator | 2 | 1 | Fill-In | Later | Provides clarity in workflows but does not reduce time |
| B6 Driver Alert Notifications | 4 | 3 | Major Project | Next | Addresses reliance on whatsapp  |
| B7 Contextual AI ETA Display | 2 | 5 | Time Sinker | Cut | Low AI adoption with limited value |
| B8 Fleet Analytics Manager View | 1 | 4 | Time Sinker | Cut | High level managers are already satisfied with the product |
| B9 Compliance Audit Trail Export | 1 | 1 | Fill-In | Cut | Does not address core moment of misery |
| B10 In-App Coordinator Training | 2 | 1 | Fill-In | Later | Helps new coordinators adopt the platform but does not address current users' issues |

## Roadmap
### NOW, Pilot (4 weeks, 3 accounts)
- **B1 One-Click Compliance Checklist**, The largest predicted benefit to compliance and reportng time
