# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** The company will lose market share to competitors that deliver a simpler product. Our customers that have frontline users that are bogged down by RouteLogic's complexity will leave for leaner tools.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is currently forced to delegate responsibility and assign tasks to different use cases within our product. Different teams have to have different procedures when interacting with the product.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must solve the threat of lean competition by addressing the complexity of our product and keeping it relevant for end users.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For end users, we will provide streamlined toolsets for different organizational functions to stave off leaner competitors targeting specific functionality. RouteLogic's product will still be best in class for high level administrative tasks since it still houses all of the functions under one product.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** RouteLogic's largest enterprise account has expressed they are trialing a leaner competitor. It is reasonable, though perhaps a bit of a leap, to infer that other customers are exploring their options.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** It's a systemic problem that affects the vast majority of end users. While the product offers a lot of functionality for high level users, the frontline users that use it every day suffer.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** I believe it removes the identified obstacle. It gives frontend users focused toolsets while maintaining the administrative complexity that high level users expect from the product.
