# GTM Launch Plan, RouteLogic (B2B)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | One click compliance checklist |
| Goal | Conversion |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Conversion, Get more dispatch coordinators to more deeply use the feature. This way, critical steps of the process are handled by RouteLogic's product
- **Target audience:** Dispatch coordinators

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, RouteLogic operates from a position of defending against leaner challengers. RouteLogic already has a captive audience of users that it is looking to improve the feature set for. There is no immediate need to grow market share. The goal with the product is to deepen existing customer engagement and satisfaction.

## Channels
1. **Owned: Email to customers with feature notes**
2. **Owned: Account coordinators giving demonstrations to customers**
3. **Earned: Conduct focus groups with dispatch coordinators and identify satisfied users for testimonials**

## Enablement & Assets
Launch email with details. Sales needs demos to tell customers about the feature. FAQs on how the one click compliance check works for support.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM owns the plan, focus groups, and performance reporting. Marketing owns the email and sales demonstration materials. Engineering owns the release. Support owns the FAQs.
- **Timeline:** Week 1: Focus groups Week 2: Email to customers Week 3: Rollout and account executives offer demonstrations to customers Week 4: monitor

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Compliance adoption among dispatch coordinators, continued usage, and time spent logging compliance checks
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Drops in adoption of the routing tools
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Iterate
